# WDDAM™ Medical Technology — Team Level/Year Certification Schedules

Reference source of truth for the Medical Technology campaign page. Each Team has one row per level (L1–L5). Each level is scheduled across a 3-year cycle. In every cell, an item is one of:

- **Complete** — credential finished in that year
- **Start** — credential begins in that year
- **Continue** — learning carries on (no completion yet)
- **Note** — competency development (e.g. HTM) with no exam

Sequence rule: CABT completes at L2, CBET at L3, CHTM at L5. The formal HTM management credential sits at L5; its underlying competencies are developed progressively across earlier levels.

### HTM competencies by level

- **L1:** equipment records, documentation, maintenance fundamentals
- **L2:** CMMS, asset inventories, work orders, maintenance scheduling
- **L3:** operational maintenance coordination, equipment availability, lifecycle records
- **L4:** maintenance governance, service performance, equipment risk
- **L5:** CHTM, strategic HTM management, financial and operational governance, workforce management, equipment lifecycle strategy
